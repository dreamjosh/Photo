About Damor Tractore Service

Trusted tractor care since 2010

Damor Tractore Service is a dedicated team of tractor mechanics and service professionals committed to keeping your farm equipment running at peak performance. Our mission is to provide transparent, reliable, and affordable service to every farmer.

With over 15 years of experience, we understand the critical role tractors play in agriculture. That's why we offer doorstep service, genuine parts, and a digital bill management system to keep you informed every step of the way.

Our Tractor Book system allows you to track all work done, payments made, and pending dues in one place — with photo documentation and PDF bills you can share instantly.
